Risotto alla Milanese

A transformation journey from simple ingredients to golden perfection

Ingredients

Arborio Rice
1½ cups
Chicken Stock
6 cups, warm
Saffron Threads
Pinch
Onion
1 medium, diced
Parmigiano
½ cup, grated
1

Prepare the Base

Bloom saffron in warm stock. Sauté diced onion in olive oil until translucent and fragrant, creating the aromatic foundation.

5 minutes
Medium heat
2

Toast the Rice

Add Arborio rice, stirring to coat each grain with oil. Toast until edges become translucent, awakening the starch for perfect creaminess.

3 minutes
Medium-high
3

Gradual Transformation

Add warm saffron stock one ladle at a time, stirring constantly. Each addition releases starch, building the signature creamy texture.

18 minutes
Gentle simmer
4

Final Mantecatura

Finish with butter and Parmigiano, stirring vigorously to create the perfect wave-like consistency that defines great risotto.

2 minutes
Off heat

Golden Perfection

A creamy, luxurious risotto with the perfect al dente bite, infused with saffron's golden hue and earthy aroma.

4 Servings
35 Minutes
★★★ Skill
