Ingredients

Chicken Thighs 1.5 lbs
Bell Peppers 2 large
Yellow Onion 1 medium
Jasmine Rice 1.5 cups
Garlic 4 cloves
Saffron 1/2 tsp
Chicken Stock 3 cups
Paprika 1 tbsp
Olive Oil 3 tbsp
Prepare Base
Heat oil, sauté onions and garlic until fragrant
5 mins
Season Chicken
Season thighs with paprika and brown all sides
8 mins
Prep Vegetables
Slice peppers, bloom saffron in warm stock
6 mins
Combine & Simmer
Add rice, stock, and vegetables. Bring to boil
3 mins
Spanish Paella
Cover and cook until rice is tender and liquid absorbed
25 mins

Traditional Spanish Rice Dish

Chef's Notes

Use bomba rice if available for authentic texture
Don't stir once rice is added - this creates the socarrat
Let rest 5 minutes before serving
Garnish with lemon wedges and fresh parsley
Pairs beautifully with Spanish Tempranillo wine
