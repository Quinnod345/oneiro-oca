Chicken Tikka Masala

⏱️ 45 min
👥 4 servings

Ingredients

Protein
2 lbs chicken breast
1 cup Greek yogurt
Vegetables
1 large onion
4 cloves garlic
2 tbsp ginger
Spices & Aromatics
2 tsp garam masala
1 tsp cumin
1 tsp paprika
Base & Liquid
1 can crushed tomatoes
1 cup heavy cream
1
Marinate
30 min
Chicken breast
Greek yogurt
Garam masala
🥄
Combine chicken with yogurt and spices. Let marinate for 30 minutes
2
Sauté Base
8 min
Onion
Garlic
Ginger
Cumin
🔥
Heat oil and sauté aromatics until fragrant and golden
3
Build Sauce
12 min
Crushed tomatoes
Paprika
Heavy cream
🍲
Add tomatoes and spices. Simmer until thickened, then stir in cream
4
Cook Chicken
15 min
Marinated chicken
🍳
Add chicken to sauce and simmer until cooked through
