Ingredients

  • Chicken Breast 2 lbs
  • Olive Oil 2 tbsp
  • Garlic 3 cloves
  • Lemon 1 whole
  • Herbs mixed
🔪

Preparation

  • Season chicken with salt and pepper
  • Mince garlic cloves finely
  • Zest and juice the lemon
  • Chop fresh herbs
  • Let chicken marinate 15 minutes
🔥

Cooking

  • Heat oil in large skillet
  • Sear chicken 6-8 minutes per side
  • Add garlic and herbs to pan
  • Deglaze with lemon juice
  • Simmer until sauce reduces
🍽

Plating

  • Rest chicken 5 minutes
  • Slice chicken diagonally
  • Arrange on warmed plates
  • Drizzle with pan sauce
  • Garnish with fresh herbs
✨

Final Dish

🍗
Herb-Crusted Lemon Chicken
