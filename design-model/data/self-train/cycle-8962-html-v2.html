Heritage Vanilla Cake

Reconstructed from fragments, circa 1947

Artifact Inventory

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1½ cups granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs, room temperature
  • ½ cup melted butter
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup whole milk

Reconstruction Method

Preheat excavation chamber to 350°F (175°C). Prepare a 9-inch specimen container with butter and flour.

Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt. Document dry mixture composition.

Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add eggs individually, incorporating each fully. Add vanilla extract.

Alternate adding dry ingredients and milk, beginning and ending with flour mixture. Mix until just combined.

Transfer to prepared container. Bake for 30-35 minutes until golden and springy to touch.
