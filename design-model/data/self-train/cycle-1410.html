Mushroom Risotto

⏱ Total: 45 min 👥 Serves: 4 🔥 Difficulty: Medium

🥘 Ingredients

Arborio Rice 1 cup
Mixed Mushrooms 8 oz
Vegetable Broth 4 cups
White Wine ½ cup
Onion 1 medium
Garlic 3 cloves
Parmesan ½ cup
Butter 3 tbsp
Olive Oil 2 tbsp
Fresh Thyme 1 tsp
1

Prepare Base

Warm broth in a separate pot. Dice onion finely and mince garlic.

Broth Onion Garlic
⏱ 5 min
2

Sauté Mushrooms

Heat oil in large pan. Cook sliced mushrooms until golden and moisture evaporates.

Mushrooms Oil Thyme
⏱ 8 min
3

Toast Rice

Add butter, onion, and garlic. Cook until fragrant. Add rice and toast for 2 minutes.

Rice Butter Onion Mix
⏱ 5 min
4

Deglaze & Simmer

Add wine and stir until absorbed. Gradually add warm broth, stirring constantly.

Wine Warm Broth
⏱ 20 min
5

Finish & Combine

Fold in sautéed mushrooms and grated Parmesan. Adjust seasoning and consistency.

Mushrooms Parmesan Butter
⏱ 7 min
🍚

Creamy Mushroom Risotto

Rich, creamy arborio rice with earthy mushrooms, finished with Parmesan and fresh herbs.
