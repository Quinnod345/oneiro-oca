Capon in Galyntyne

From The Forme of Cury, 1390

Season a whole chicken with salt and pepper. Roast at 375°F for 1 hour until golden.

For the sauce: Sauté 2 minced shallots in butter. Add ½ cup white wine, ¼ cup vinegar, and 2 tbsp honey.

Mix in ½ tsp each of cinnamon, ginger, and a pinch of cloves. Simmer until reduced by half.

Strain the sauce and serve over the carved chicken. Garnish with fresh herbs.
