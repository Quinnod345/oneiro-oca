Spring Asparagus Risotto

A celebration of early seasonal vegetables

Main Ingredients

1½ cups Arborio rice
1 bunch Fresh asparagus, trimmed
4 cups Vegetable stock, warm
½ cup Dry white wine
¾ cup Freshly grated Parmesan

Aromatics

1 small Shallot, finely diced
2 cloves Garlic, minced
2 tbsp Fresh lemon juice

Preparation

1

Blanch asparagus in salted boiling water for 2 minutes. Reserve cooking water and chop asparagus into 1-inch pieces.

2

In a large pan, sauté shallot in olive oil until translucent. Add garlic and rice, stirring until grains are coated.

3

Add wine and stir until absorbed. Gradually add warm stock, one ladle at a time, stirring constantly until each addition is absorbed.

4

After 15 minutes, fold in asparagus pieces. Continue cooking until rice is creamy and al dente. Remove from heat, stir in Parmesan and lemon juice.
