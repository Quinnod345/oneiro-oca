Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g pancetta, diced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 100g pecorino romano
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Key Steps

  • 1 Boil pasta in salted water
  • 2 Render pancetta until crispy
  • 3 Whisk eggs with cheese
  • 4 Combine everything off heat
Prep Time 10 min
