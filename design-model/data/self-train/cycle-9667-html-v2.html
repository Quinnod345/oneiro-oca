Spiced Honey Cakes

Take good honey and clarify it on the fire, and take fine white bread crumbs and grate them well. Mix the bread with the honey until it is stiff enough to be shaped.

Add thereto powder of ginger, long pepper, and cinnamon, with a portion of saffron to give it noble color. Work this together while warm.

Form into small cakes and let them dry before the fire, turning them often so they harden evenly. These shall keep through winter if stored properly.
