Raw Materials

Fresh Salmon
1 lb fillet
skin removed, pin bones checked
Arborio Rice
1½ cups
rinsed until water runs clear
Fish Stock
4 cups
kept warm, seasoned
Shallots
2 medium
finely minced
White Wine
½ cup
dry, room temperature
Fresh Dill
3 tbsp
chopped, stems removed

Transformation Process

1
Foundation Building
Sauté minced shallots in olive oil until translucent and aromatic, creating the flavor base.
3-4 minutes
2
Rice Awakening
Toast arborio rice with shallots until edges become translucent, awakening the starches.
2 minutes
3
Wine Integration
Deglaze with white wine, stirring until completely absorbed, adding complexity.
3 minutes
4
Gradual Enrichment
Add warm stock one ladle at a time, stirring constantly until each addition is absorbed.
18-20 minutes
5
Salmon Integration
Gently fold in salmon pieces and dill, cooking until fish flakes easily.
4-5 minutes
6
Final Harmony
Finish with butter and lemon zest, adjusting seasoning for perfect balance.
1 minute

Final Creation

Salmon & Dill Risotto
A harmonious blend where individual ingredients have transformed into a unified, creamy masterpiece. The salmon provides protein richness while the arborio rice delivers luxurious texture. Fresh dill adds brightness that cuts through the richness, creating perfect balance.
