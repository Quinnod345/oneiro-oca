Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon Herb Butter

4 servings • Created by Chef Marie
25 Minutes
4 Servings
★ 4.8 Rating
Prep
4 salmon fillets (6oz each)
2 lemons, juiced & zested
4 tbsp butter
Fresh herbs (dill, parsley)
Salt & pepper
2 tbsp olive oil
1
Pat salmon dry and season with salt and pepper
2
Chop herbs finely and zest lemons
8 min
Cook
3
Heat oil in large skillet over medium-high heat
4
Sear salmon skin-side up for 4-5 minutes
5
Flip and cook 3-4 minutes more
10 min
Combine
6
Remove salmon, reduce heat to low
7
Add butter, lemon juice, and herbs to pan
8
Swirl to create herb butter sauce
5 min
Finish
9
Return salmon to pan briefly
10
Spoon herb butter over fillets
Pan-Seared Salmon
Perfectly cooked salmon with a golden crust, topped with aromatic lemon herb butter sauce
2 min
