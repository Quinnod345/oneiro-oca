Adaptive Root Vegetable Stew

2 cups winter squash, cubed
3 cups summer tomatoes, fresh
1 pound parsnips, roasted
1 pound zucchini, grilled
4-6 cups vegetable stock
1 bunch fresh basil
2 sprigs rosemary, dried
1

Begin by warming oil in a heavy pot. 20-30 min for root vegetables to soften, adjusting for seasonal density.

2

Layer ingredients according to current moon phase—densest at bottom during waning, lighter during waxing. 15 min

3

Simmer until vegetables surrender their essence. 45-90 min depending on barometric pressure.
