Classic Beef Bourguignon
1

Preparation & Mise en Place

Gather and prepare all ingredients. Cut beef into chunks, dice vegetables, and organize aromatics.

2 lbs beef chuck
6 oz bacon
1 onion, diced
2 carrots
8 oz mushrooms
3 garlic cloves
Knife Skills
2

Browning & Flavor Building

Render bacon fat, sear beef chunks until golden, and caramelize vegetables to develop deep flavors through Maillard reactions.

Seared beef
Rendered bacon
Caramelized onions
Browned carrots
High Heat Searing
3

Deglazing & Braising

Deglaze with red wine to capture fond, add herbs and stock, then slow-braise to transform tough collagen into silky gelatin.

Red wine reduction
Beef stock
Thyme bundle
Bay leaves
Low & Slow Braising
4

Final Integration

Add sautéed mushrooms, adjust seasoning, and reduce sauce to perfect consistency. All elements merge into harmony.

Sautéed mushrooms
Reduced sauce
Fresh herbs
Seasoning
Sauce Reduction
