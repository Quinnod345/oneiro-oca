Beef Bourguignon Timeline

Total Time: 3.5 hours Serves: 6 Difficulty: Intermediate

Fresh Ingredients

2 lbs beef chuck
1 bottle red wine
8 oz bacon
1 lb carrots
1 lb pearl onions
8 oz mushrooms
Prep

Initial Prep

Cut and prepare all ingredients

  • Cube beef into 2" pieces
  • Dice bacon into lardons
  • Slice carrots diagonally
  • Peel pearl onions
1
Sear

Brown & Build

Create deep flavors through browning

  • Render bacon until crispy
  • Sear beef in batches
  • Sauté aromatics
  • Deglaze with wine
2
Braise

Long Simmer

Low and slow transformation

  • Combine all elements
  • Add herbs & stock
  • Simmer 2.5 hours
  • Stir occasionally
3
Finish

Final Touch

Perfect the texture and flavor

  • Sauté mushrooms
  • Adjust seasoning
  • Thicken if needed
  • Garnish with herbs
4
🍲
