    Pixelmator Pro

    Picture perfect.

    Pixelmator Pro makes it easy to edit images and create striking graphics across Mac and iPad. It’s packed with powerful AI features that build on Apple Intelligence, and it works in concert with the other amazing apps in Apple Creator Studio.1

    Apple Creator StudioCreator Studio A suite deal for any creator.

    Pixelmator Pro is part of Apple Creator Studio, the essential collection of creative apps that includes Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, and more.

    Try it free2 Learn more

    Pixelmator Pro for Mac is also available as a one-time purchase on the App Store

    Get to know Pixelmator Pro.

    • Creative Imaging
    • Intelligent Features
    • Design
    • Templates
    • Vector Graphics
    • Round-trip Compatibility

    • An incredibly advanced yet intuitive tool for creative imaging.

    • Simplify workflows and speed up creativity with powerful intelligence features.

    • Create and edit visuals with pro-level quality — without the complexity.

    • Jump-start your ideas with beautiful, professionally designed, fully customizable templates.

    • Scale graphics to any size with a vast collection of powerful vector tools.

    • Design and edit effortlessly wherever you are, on Mac or iPad.

    Creative Imaging

    Reimagine your images.

    Explore all-new ways to express yourself with a powerful collection of creative effects, adjustments, and tools.

    • Layering for endless possibilities.

      With nondestructive editing, use effects, fills, strokes, and shadows freely, and easily revert without affecting image quality if you change your mind.

    • Over 60 versatile effects.

      Mix and match effects like Blur, Distortion, Sharpen, Tile, Halftone, and many more to get the style you want.

    • Adjust colors with incredible precision.

      Advanced tools and adjustment layers let you edit colors by range or selection, then refine individual colors to create perfectly polished palettes and tones.

    • Intelligent masking.

      The Select and Mask feature lets you detect edges — even fine details like hair or fur — and creates a clean mask for you, eliminating the tedious step of tracing subjects manually.

    • Full support for RAW photos.

      Add RAW photos as layers to edit them directly without having to convert or preprocess them.

    • Easy video retouching.

      Work with video layers the same way you do with images, making it simple to mask or crop videos, change colors, and more.

    Intelligence

    Brilliant tools for bright ideas.

    Built-in intelligence helps handle time-consuming tasks, so you’re free to focus on what you want to create.

    • Scale with stunning clarity.

      Super Resolution analyzes your image and automatically scales up the resolution so it stays sharp. And it can even enhance details so your image looks just as clear when it’s bigger.

    • Remove backgrounds with just a click.

      Effortlessly isolate your subject or hide the background with a mask you can adjust anytime.

    • Quickly improve image quality.

      With Deband, you can smooth out gradients and remove compression artifacts while keeping smaller details intact.

    • Auto Enhance.

      Get a head start with photo edits that are automatically applied, then refine further with your own ideas.

    • Find the best crop, fast.

      Auto Crop suggests the best way to frame your photo and Auto Straighten levels it out for you. You can also pick from ready-made sizes to fit your photo’s aspect ratio.

    Retouching

    Make every shot shine.

    Tap into incredible tools to fine-tune your photos and instantly remove distractions for a perfect, polished look.

    • Repair tool.

      Quickly remove dust, blemishes, distracting details — even entire objects — to perfect your images.

    • Clone stamp tool.

      Easily copy pixels to remove distractions, repair areas, or replicate textures, so you can get the image you imagined.

    • Smarter selection tools.

      Select a subject with ease. Or use a wide range of tools that allow you to isolate and edit specific areas with a single click.

    • Brush-based retouching tools.

      Refine parts of your image with brushstrokes that brighten, darken, sharpen, soften, saturate, desaturate, and more.

    • Selective Clarity.

      Enhance your image precisely by adjusting clarity and texture in shadows, midtones, and highlights.

    • Intelligent Denoise.

      Remove camera noise from photos faster and more easily than ever.

    Design

    The dazzle’s in the details.

    Harness the power of layer-based editing, a customizable workspace, and broad file support to bring more flexibility to your workflows.

    • Layer-based editing.

      Organize your visual creations into layers for easier workflows, and make nondestructive changes and edits to complex projects.

    • Warp tool.

      Push and pull a specific area of an image in any direction. Fix subtle imperfections or dramatically transform any image.

    • Smart Guides.

      The Arrange tool quickly and intelligently snaps your layers into position as you move them around. It even lets you align and distribute layers automatically.

    • Beautiful templates and mockups.

      Start working from stunning templates, including content for social media and print. Drag and drop images onto shirts, cups, or other predefined objects, to help you visualize the real‑life look and feel of your designs.

    • Customizable workspace.

      Move sidebars, reimagine your list of tools, choose one of the built-in workspaces, or create your own.

    • Broad file support.

      Pixelmator Pro supports a wide range of raster, vector, video, and photo files, including over 750 RAW formats.

    Vector Graphics

    Built to scale.

    Pixelmator Pro supports many popular vector file formats and includes a large collection of easily customizable Smart Shapes to make your designs shine at any scale.

    • Premade shapes.

      Add a variety of ready-to-use shapes to your compositions and adjust their size, color, and style in seconds.

    • Draw shapes from scratch.

      The Pen tool lets you precisely draw vector shapes and paths by adding sharp or smooth anchor points. And you can draw freely, just as you do with a pencil on paper.

    • Path Type tool.

      Let your text follow any shape or use Circular Type to create perfectly curved and circular text.

    • Typography design.

      Customize typefaces, sizes, and colors. Adjust emphasis, refine alignment and spacing, and tap into advanced typography features like ligatures and glyphs.

    • Vector file format support.

      Pixelmator Pro supports PSD, SVG, PDF, Adobe Illustrator, and Illustrator EPS, so you can edit shapes and paths in any of these files, then export them while keeping their vector data.

    iPad

    Make it on the move.

    Pixelmator Pro on iPad features Multi-Touch control and Apple Pencil support for hands-on creativity, and the light, compact iPad design makes it easy to take your work anywhere.

    With on-canvas controls and a fully customizable workspace, you’ll have just what you need to create desktop-grade designs using familiar gestures like tap, pinch, and swipe.

    Native support for Apple Pencil gives you precision edits and pressure sensitivity so that you can create naturally.3

    Creative Imaging with Apple

    Aligned by design.

    Get the best of both Mac and iPad with Pixelmator Pro. Enjoy the blazing-fast performance of Apple silicon whenever inspiration strikes.

    Switch between working on Mac and iPad — with every edit seamlessly synced across devices.4

    Easily copy layers on your Mac and paste them on iPad — or vice versa — using Universal Clipboard.

    Use one keyboard and mouse across your Mac and iPad, drag files between screens, and keep edits in sync as you move between devices.

    Harness the full power of Pixelmator Pro right in the Photos app — turning it into a complete editing studio where you can work on libraries of photos, organize albums, and more.

Final Cut Pro, Motion, and Compressor give you smarter, faster ways to craft and cut videos. Pixelmator Pro lets you easily edit images and create striking graphics. Logic Pro and MainStage help you produce, perform, and perfect your music. And while Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform remain free for everyone, an Apple Creator Studio subscription offers premium templates, a library of high-quality, royalty-free photos and graphics, and powerful intelligence features.

    • A subscription to Apple Creator Studio bundles Apple’s powerful creative apps for one low monthly payment.

      The Apple Creator Studio version of Pixelmator Pro includes access to the Warp Tool feature. A one-time-purchase version of Pixelmator Pro is also available for Mac on the App Store.

    • Pixelmator Pro is included in your subscription to Apple Creator Studio, which also includes Logic Pro and Final Cut Pro, along with other powerfully intelligent tools. You can try Apple Creator Studio free for 30 days. After your free trial, new subscribers pay $12.99 per month or $129 per year. Educator and student subscriptions are $2.99 per month or $29.99 per year.

      Pixelmator Pro for Mac is also available as a one-time purchase for $49.99.

    • Check out the Pixelmator Pro User Guide here for Mac, and here for iPad.

    • Photomator remains available as a separate purchase from the App Store.

    • Pixelmator Pro for iPad is available as part of an Apple Creator Studio subscription, alongside the Mac version and other pro apps like Final Cut Pro and Logic Pro. It brings all the features that Pixelmator Pro users love on Mac to iPad, including nondestructive editing, AI features, tools for freely transforming layers, and more — all optimized for touch.

      Pixelmator Classic for iOS, released in 2014 as a companion app to the now-discontinued Pixelmator Classic for Mac, provides basic image editing features such as cropping, color adjustments, and effects. It remains a functional app but is no longer being updated.

    • Pixelmator Pro is available on macOS 26 or iPadOS 26.

      Pixelmator Pro for iPad requires an iPad with Apple M1 chip or later, iPad (A16), or iPad mini (A17 Pro).
