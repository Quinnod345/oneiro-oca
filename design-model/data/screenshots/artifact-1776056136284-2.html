Notes on Writing Well
April 13, 2026 · 3 min read

Good writing is clear thinking made visible. The hardest part isn't grammar or punctuation — it's the willingness to sit with an idea long enough to understand it.

Writers who struggle usually struggle with courage, not craft. They hedge, pad, and retreat behind passive constructions when the subject becomes uncomfortable.

A great sentence ends like setting something on a table — firm, final, done. You can always reach for --verbose when context demands it, but the default is silence.
