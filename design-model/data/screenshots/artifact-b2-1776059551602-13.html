Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?
+

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. To start a return, contact our support team with your order number and we'll guide you through the process.

How long does shipping take?
+

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Expedited shipping (2–3 days) and overnight options are available at checkout. Orders placed before 2pm EST ship the same day.

Do you offer customer support?
+

Yes — our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm EST. You can reach us by email or live chat. We aim to respond to all inquiries within one business day.

Can I change or cancel my order?
+

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that window, the order enters fulfillment and changes may not be possible. Contact support immediately if you need to make adjustments.
